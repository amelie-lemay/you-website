Book Synopsis

Jane, like every friend and foe around her, woke up in this vast and empty world absent of memories. Some had been here for a year, five years, ten years - the oldest tales dated to twenty! - and herself had wandered for five months. Jane had had the luck to meet the clan that took her home early, where she was safe, fed, and appreciated. She loved her group, she really did, yet could not share the same hatred as everyone for the pack living across the lake. She hated this ever-going war. And, above all, Jane could not take her eyes off the horizon singing freedom.

About This Website

Hi, this is the author! I created this website as a companion to your reading experience. Initially meant for additional information on a specific chapter (this spoiler-free version will change once you reach this point!)a translation of French dialogues for non-speakers of the language, I've expanded it to include my sketches of Jane's maps and other notes on characters and places you might find interesting. And, to top it all, it is designed to adapt to your reading progress so you can navigate through this site with no fear of accidentally uncovering an important plot line. Thank you for taking an interest in my story, and happy reading!
